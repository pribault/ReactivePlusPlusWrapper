Detailed Description

Copyable cancellation scope that disposes its attached children together.

Copies share the scope. Destruction releases ownership without requesting cancellation of children. A moved-from scope is empty and considered disposed.

Note
add(), remove(), dispose() and isDisposed() use thread-safe RPP operations. Concurrent assignment or destruction of the same handle object is not supported. Concurrent remove() and dispose() may cancel the child if disposal wins the race.
